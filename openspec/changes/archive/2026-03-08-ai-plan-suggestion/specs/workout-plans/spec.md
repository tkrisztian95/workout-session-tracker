## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Plans tab has an AI Suggest entry point

The Plans tab SHALL display an "AI Suggest" button alongside the primary plan creation action. The button SHALL be visible regardless of the number of existing plans.

#### Scenario: AI Suggest button visible on Plans tab

- **WHEN** the user navigates to the Plans tab
- **THEN** an "AI Suggest" button SHALL be visible on the screen

#### Scenario: AI Suggest button opens suggestion modal

- **WHEN** the user taps the "AI Suggest" button
- **THEN** the AI plan suggestion modal SHALL open
